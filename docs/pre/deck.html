Study design · Three-minute games with no increment

Three forecasts use the same moves and different inputs

The verified sample contains 839,176 labelled moves from 11,253 games.

Position and ratings

Both players’ ratings, the engine’s evaluation and board features such as material and legal moves.

Add both clocks

Each player’s remaining seconds when the turn starts, and the mover’s share of their combined time.

Add recent time use

Time the mover spent on their previous turn and their average over up to three preceding turns.

Games came from snowball sampling through active players and their opponents and required engine annotations.
