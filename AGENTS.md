# DSC5001 · Beyond the Board

The group studies whether information available when a chess player starts a turn improves the predicted probability of a serious error on the next move.

## Working files

- Read [handoff.md](handoff.md) and the [Chinese progress log](docs/Project_Progress.md) before resuming work.
- The [evaluation protocol](experiment/evaluation.md) defines the splits, label and comparisons. Add each completed or invalidated run to [the experiment ledger](experiment/results.md).
- The input table and its provenance belong in [data](data/README_data.md). Preserve the distributed `data/moves.csv.gz` for reproducing the original group results.
- Keep the team's original metrics and figures under `results/`. Write follow-up experiments to named subdirectories and link their results from the progress log.
- The editable report and compiled PDF are under `docs/report/`. Identify the cohort and evaluation period for every performance table.

For supported formats, use Microsoft MarkItDown first to read or convert source documents. Check the original when headings, figures, equations, pagination or layout matter.
